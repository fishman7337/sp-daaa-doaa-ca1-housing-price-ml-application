# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

EstateScope AI is an academic engineering project. Contributors and reviewers are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, and keep feedback focused on the work.

Expected behavior:

- Be clear, constructive, and professional.
- Credit sources, collaborators, and prior work.
- Avoid personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.
- Treat data, credentials, and student records with care.
- Raise risks early when a change may affect security, privacy, fairness, or reproducibility.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or exclusionary conduct.
- Publishing secrets, private data, or assessment materials without permission.
- Plagiarism, fabricated results, or hidden use of unapproved data.
- Intentionally breaking tests, bypassing review, or misrepresenting model performance.

## Reporting

For this CA submission, concerns should be raised with the project owner first where appropriate. Academic integrity or safety concerns should follow Singapore Polytechnic module guidance and escalation routes.

## Enforcement

Reports should be handled proportionately. Possible responses include clarification, requested changes, removal of harmful content, or escalation to module staff.
